# ORVESTRA Product Registry Governance

This document defines the governance rules for the ORVESTRA Product Registry.

The registry exists to maintain a public record of ORVESTRA knowledge products without exposing protected licensed content.

## Governance Authority

Registry Owner: ORVESTRA  
Managed System: MaMeeFarm.online  
Registry Type: Knowledge Product Registry  
Visibility: Public  
Status: Active

## Registry Function

The registry may record:

- Knowledge ID
- product title
- producer
- department
- primary domain
- domain code
- content type
- product type
- published date
- version
- record status
- access status
- rights status
- product description
- source record information
- evidence status
- CID where approved for public disclosure

## Public Disclosure Boundary

The public registry records the existence, classification, status, provenance, and rights information of a knowledge product.

The registry does not automatically disclose:

- full licensed product content
- private source files
- internal working records
- unpublished evidence packages
- internal analytical methods
- internal governance logic
- restricted operational information

Only information approved for public disclosure may be added to this repository.

## Product Registration

Each registered knowledge product must receive a unique Knowledge ID.

Knowledge IDs must not be reused.

A registered record must preserve its assigned identifier even if the product is later superseded, withdrawn, archived, or replaced.

## Record Status

Product records may use approved status values defined by the registry.

Changes in status must be recorded without changing the identity of the original Knowledge ID.

Historical records should remain traceable.

## Evidence References

CID, OTS status, or other evidence references may be recorded when they are approved for public disclosure.

The existence of an evidence reference does not grant access to private evidence files or protected product content.

## Rights Control

Registration in this repository does not transfer ownership or grant unrestricted use of any product.

## Managed Distribution

MaMeeFarm.online is the managed distribution system for applicable ORVESTRA knowledge products.

Product access conditions, licensing conditions, membership requirements, pricing, or delivery controls may be managed separately from this public registry.

## Record Integrity

Registry records should remain consistent with their approved source records.

Public records must not be modified in a way that creates a false production history, changes the identity of a product, or removes required rights information.

Corrections and updates should preserve traceability to the original record.
