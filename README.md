# ORVESTRA Product Registry

Public registry of ORVESTRA knowledge products.

This repository records knowledge products that have been produced, approved, registered, and prepared for controlled distribution through MaMeeFarm.online.

The registry is intended to provide a public record of product existence, classification, production status, rights status, and available evidence references without exposing the full licensed content.

## Repository Purpose

This repository is used to:

- register completed ORVESTRA knowledge products
- maintain Knowledge IDs
- record product titles and classifications
- identify the producing department
- record publication and approval status
- maintain product evidence references
- record CID and OTS status where applicable
- document rights and licensing status
- provide traceability between registered products and MaMeeFarm.online

## Public Registry Scope

This repository may contain:

- product records
- product metadata
- product type definitions
- public registry information
- approved evidence references
- rights and license information
- product status records

Full licensed product content is not required to be stored in this public repository.

## Product Identification

Registered knowledge products use the following identifier structure:

`ORV-KNW-0001`

Where:

`ORV` identifies ORVESTRA.  
`KNW` identifies a Knowledge Product.  
The numerical sequence identifies the individual registered product.
